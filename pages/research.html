Research

I want to build adaptive intelligence: systems that bring understanding and generation together, and adapt in real time through continuous interaction.

My work explores parts of this question through feedback-driven image editing, brain dynamics, and agents that use structured knowledge.

Selected research

Recursive Image Editing

Research in progress · mlPC Lab, UC San Diego · Advisor: Prof. Zhuowen Tu

An image editor that learns to repair its own failed edits, using feedback from a vision-language critic, its previous attempt, and the source image.

My work: training the editor (LoRA, flow matching, and on-policy RL) and building the editing dataset.

  • Post-training
  • RL
  • Data generation pipeline

BrainMech: A Mechanistic Foundation Model for Human Brain Dynamics Across Lifespan

Jiaqi Wu, Shuheng Cao, Tingting Dan, Ziquan Wei, Zhixuan Zhou, Guorong Wu

Manuscript · 2026 · ACM Lab, UNC Chapel Hill · Advisor: Prof. Guorong Wu

A Koopman-structured fMRI foundation model for learning and analyzing latent brain dynamics.

My work: the self-supervised pretraining objective, functional connectivity preprocessing, and region-level mediation analyses.

  • Pre-training
  • Foundation model
  • Dynamics modeling
